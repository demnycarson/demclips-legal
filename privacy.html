DemClips Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

1. Overview

DemClips is a personal tool that uploads edited video clips to its operator's own TikTok account as drafts. This policy explains what information DemClips handles and how.

2. Information we collect

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, DemClips receives:

  • Basic TikTok profile information (account ID and display name), used to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token, used to upload videos to that account.

DemClips does not collect passwords, contacts, messages, location, payment details, or information about any other TikTok users.

3. How information is used

This information is used only to upload videos to the connected TikTok account as drafts. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. They are never uploaded to any other server.

5. Sharing

DemClips does not sell, rent, or share personal information with anyone. Videos and upload requests are sent only to TikTok through its official API.

6. Your choices and data deletion

You can revoke DemClips' access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. To have stored tokens deleted, email the address below and they will be deleted within 30 days.

7. Children

DemClips is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
